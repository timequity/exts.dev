Privacy details

Overview

TabZen helps you manage tabs with auto-save, named sessions, and search.

To deliver that, it needs access to your open tabs and local browser storage, but not to any external service.

Permissions Explained

  • storage stores sessions, settings, and recovery data locally on your device.
  • tabs reads your open tabs so the extension can save sessions and power search.
  • alarms runs the local auto-save schedule every 5 minutes.

Local Storage Only

All TabZen data is stored in chrome.storage.local.

  • No automatic sync across devices by default.
  • No cloud backend or server-side session copies.
  • No access for us to inspect your saved tabs.

Network Activity

TabZen makes zero network requests. There is no sync service, analytics endpoint, or remote account layer.

That is intentional: the safest tab data is the data that never leaves your browser.

What we do not do

✓ We do not collect browsing analytics or usage metrics.
✓ We do not send your tabs or sessions to external servers.
✓ We do not require an account for core functionality.
✓ We do not inject ads or sponsored suggestions.
✓ We do not sell data because there is no backend data store.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. Given the product direction, significant privacy changes are unlikely.

Questions
