How it Works

GrayMode helps you focus by removing color from distracting websites, reducing dopamine triggers and making them less engaging.

Enable it for specific sites, keep it ready for work hours, and make visual temptation boring enough to ignore.

Troubleshooting

Grayscale not applying?

  • Refresh the page after enabling the extension.
  • Make sure GrayMode is enabled in the popup.
  • Verify the site is included in your selected list or category.
  • Some sites with strict CSP can block visual filters.
